JUL — AUG 2026 · GITHUB.COM/NEKOPUNK44/QUANT · TS + PYTHON MONOREPO
  • The statistics that guard against overfitting are written from scratch in TypeScript, with no external libraries: Deflated Sharpe, Probabilistic Sharpe corrected for skewness and kurtosis, PBO, Monte Carlo. The backtest core has zero runtime dependencies.
  • Walk-forward: parameters fitted on the train window, scored on an untouched test window, the out-of-sample curve stitched across folds.
